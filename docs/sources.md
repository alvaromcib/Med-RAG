# Source documents

The guidelines are copyrighted and are **not** included in this repository. Download them from the publishers and
place them in `data/` (sub-folders become the `category` metadata):

| Folder | Document | Where to get it |
|---|---|---|
| `01_Imaging_Protocols` | ACR Appropriateness Criteria® Cerebrovascular Disease (2017 revision) | [acsearch.acr.org](https://acsearch.acr.org/docs/69478/narrative/) |
| `01_Imaging_Protocols` | ACR Appropriateness Criteria® Headache | [acsearch.acr.org](https://acsearch.acr.org/list) (search "Headache") |
| `02_Classification` | Louis DN et al. The 2021 WHO Classification of Tumors of the CNS: a summary. *Neuro-Oncology* 2021;23(8):1231–51 | [academic.oup.com](https://academic.oup.com/neuro-oncology/article/23/8/1231/6311214) |
| `03_Treatment` | Stupp R et al. High-grade glioma: ESMO Clinical Practice Guidelines. *Ann Oncol* 2014;25(S3):iii93–101 | [esmo.org](https://www.esmo.org/guidelines/esmo-clinical-practice-guideline-high-grade-malignant-glioma) |
| `03_Treatment` | van den Bent MJ et al. Updated EANO guideline on rational molecular testing of gliomas… Update 1. *Neuro-Oncology* 2025 | [PMC11812046](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11812046/) |

The NCCN Guidelines were part of an earlier version and were removed: their terms of use forbid using the content with any artificial intelligence model or tool.
